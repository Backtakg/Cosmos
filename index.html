Our Most Recent Research

Research Activities Cosmos College has competitive research grants from University Grants Commission (UCG) and Pokhara University Research Center (PURC). Cosmos is the only private academic institution to be awarded faculty research grant (PURC FRG 2075/75) for two research projects in engineering-cluster from PURC. Similarly, faculties of Cosmos College has been awarded with a UGC faculty research grant 2074/75.

Information Technology(IT)

Information Technology(IT)

Our recent research in Information Technology at Cosmos College focused on artificial intelligence applications in healthcare. Through data analysis and machine learning techniques, we developed predictive models to assist in early disease detection and personalized treatment recommendations, showcasing the potential of AI in revolutionizing healthcare delivery.

BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

In BBA research at Cosmos College, our study centered on consumer behavior in e-commerce platforms. Through surveys and behavioral analytics, we examined factors influencing purchasing decisions online, providing insights for businesses to optimize marketing strategies and enhance customer engagement in digital marketplaces.

Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering

In our recent research in Civil Engineering at Cosmos College, we investigated innovative sustainable construction materials. Our study explored the environmental impact and structural integrity of these materials, aiming to enhance construction practices for greater sustainability and resilience in infrastructure projects.

Scholarships at Cosmos College of Management & Technology

At Cosmos College of Management & Technology, we believe in providing opportunities for talented and deserving students to pursue their education without financial barriers. To support our students, we offer a range of scholarships to help them achieve their academic and professional goals.

Available Scholarships

There are three main categories of scholarships available:

Full Scholarships

We adhere to the norms and standards set by Pokhara University for full scholarships. These scholarships cover the full tuition fees for the duration of the program and are awarded based on specific criteria established by the university.

Limited Scholarships

Merit-Based Scholarships: Awarded to students who demonstrate exceptional academic performance and achievements. Scholarships for Female/Dalit/Underprivileged Areas: Aimed at supporting students from marginalized communities and promoting diversity and inclusion within our student body.

Semester-Wise Performance Based

These scholarships are awarded to students who excel in their academic performance each semester. Scholarships are based on the Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA), with the scholarship amount shared if multiple students achieve the same SGPA.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Cosmos College of Management and Technology (CCMT) is recognized for its quality education, own permanent campus, modern infrastructure, updated curricula, experienced professors and industry experts, excellent university results, industry collaborations, and vibrant student life. For more than 25 years, Cosmos has been committed to preparing students with the knowledge, skills, and values required to excel in their professional careers and contribute to society.
Students choose Cosmos because of its student-centered learning environment, modern teaching methods, updated curricula, experienced faculty, smart classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, industry exposure, active student clubs, sports facilities, and strong academic support. The college focuses not only on academic excellence but also on innovation, leadership, and holistic development.

All programs are regularly updated to meet emerging industry trends and global educational standards.
Civil Engineering at Cosmos combines strong theoretical foundations with practical learning through laboratories, field visits, surveying camps, design projects, and modern engineering software. Students gain knowledge in structural engineering, transportation, geotechnical engineering, water resources, environmental engineering, and construction management while learning from experienced professors and professionals.
The Computer Engineering and Information Technology programs are designed to prepare students for the digital era. Updated curricula, practical laboratory work, project-based learning, and exposure to emerging technologies help students build strong technical and problem-solving skills. Architecture at Cosmos offers a creative and inspiring environment where students develop design skills, creativity, and professional competence through studio-based learning, workshops, exhibitions, site visits, and interaction with practicing architects.
